Toolbelt Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Toolbelt for Android (app.toolbelt.toolbelt)

In short: Toolbelt has no account, no sign-in and no analytics of its own, and we run no servers that receive your data. What your phone's sensors, camera and microphone measure is processed on the phone and never uploaded. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ads software collects some device data to show them, as described below. Toolbelt Pro has no ads.

This policy explains what the Toolbelt app ("Toolbelt", "the app", "we") does with information on your device. It is published by Weaversol LLC, which you can reach at admin@weaversol.com.

1. What stays on your phone

Toolbelt is a set of everyday tools: measuring, converting, calculating, scanning, recording, notes, reminders and similar. Everything you create or measure with them stays on your device:

  • Sensor, camera and microphone readings (compass, level, sound meter, scanner, magnifier and the like) are processed on the device and never sent anywhere by us.
  • Your content (notes, to-dos, reminders, recordings, scans, saved tools, history and settings) is stored in the app's own storage on the device, or in your photo gallery or files when you choose to save it there.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything stored in the app's own storage. Files you saved to your gallery or folders stay until you delete them.

2. Permissions

A tool asks for a permission only when you open it, and you can refuse or withdraw any of them in Settings › Permissions or in Android settings. The tool then tells you it can't work instead of showing made-up data. Permissions are used on the device only:

  • Camera: QR and document scanning, magnifier, flashlight, AR distance measuring.
  • Microphone: sound meter, voice recorder, walkie-talkie and Wi-Fi calls.
  • Location: GPS tools (speed, altitude, route tracking, Qibla direction) and, as Android requires, listing nearby Wi-Fi networks. Your location is not sent to us or to advertisers by the app.
  • Physical activity: step counting.
  • Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC and phone state: the scanners and signal tools, which read what is around you and show it on screen.
  • Notifications and alarms: timers, reminders and medicine and water reminders, which are scheduled on the device.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Toolbelt shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC, or Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

DataWhat it is
Device identifiersYour device's advertising ID and app set ID.
IP addressYour device's internet address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location (city or region, not your precise position).
Ad interactionsApp launches, ad views, taps and video views.
DiagnosticsPerformance data such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is governed by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive this data about you individually; AdMob gives us only aggregated reports.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Toolbelt's Settings › Ad privacy choices. If you decline personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less data.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, under Privacy › Ads (the wording varies by device).
  • No ads. Toolbelt Pro shows no ads, and the app makes no ad requests for Pro users.

4. Toolbelt Pro purchases

Toolbelt Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms; we never see your card or billing details. The app asks Google Play on the device whether you subscribe, and keeps only that answer, on the device. No purchase data is sent to us. You can manage or cancel the subscription in the Play Store's Payments & subscriptions.

5. Services some tools contact

Most tools work fully offline. A few need the internet for what they do, and contact these services only while you use them. Like any internet request, each one shares your device's IP address with that service. None of them receives your readings, content or identity from us.

  • Currency converter: downloads exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com).
  • Speed test: downloads and uploads test data with Cloudflare's speed-test service (speed.cloudflare.com).
  • Translator: downloads Google ML Kit language models once; translation then runs on the device.
  • Maps in the Distance Meter and Route Tracker: load map images from OpenStreetMap, which learns the area of the map being viewed.
  • AR distance measuring: runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Document Scanner's "Read handwriting in Google Lens": only when you tap it, hands that one page to the Google app, which sends it to Google.
  • Links you open (a scanned web address, a location shared to a maps app, the Play Store page) open in another app, under that app's privacy policy.

Walkie-Talkie, Wi-Fi Calling and File Transfer connect only to devices on your own local network; nothing they send passes through us.

6. What we don't do

  • We have no account system and collect no names, email addresses or contacts.
  • We run no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't receive personal information about you that we could sell.

7. Children

Toolbelt is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child's data has reached Google's ad services through the app, contact us and we will help you reach Google.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, the data the app keeps is entirely under your control on your device. For ad data, use the ad choices above, or Google's tools at My Ad Center; Google handles requests about the data it collects. You can also contact us with any question.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
